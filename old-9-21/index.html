What Is a Duck?

Ducks are birds that are commonly found near lakes, rivers, ponds, and wetlands.

They are famous for swimming, waddling, flying, and saying QUACK!

Why Ducks Are Awesome

  • They can swim.
  • They can fly.
  • They can walk.
  • They have waterproof feathers.
  • They say quack.

Famous Duck Species

Here are a few ducks you might encounter:

  • Mallard
  • Wood Duck
  • Canvasback
  • American Black Duck
  • Northern Pintail

Important Duck Fact

Geese are just long ducks.
